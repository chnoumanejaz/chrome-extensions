FormPilot Settings

All saved data stays locally in your browser. Passwords, cards and other sensitive fields are skipped unless you choose to encrypt them.

Where to show FormPilot

    A site also covers its subdomains: example.com includes app.example.com. Ports, paths and a leading www. are ignored.

    Forms without a <form> tag

    Some sites build their forms from plain divs. FormPilot can detect those too, but it has to guess, so it only does so on the sites you list below. A group of fields counts only if it has at least two fields and a sign-in / save / submit style button, and it can't contain a real <form>.

    Detect on these sites

      Starts with localhost only. Sites also have to be allowed under "Where to show FormPilot".

      Sensitive fields

      Passwords, card numbers, one-time codes and secret-looking tokens. This is the default when saving a preset; you can still choose differently for each save.

      Profiles

      A profile holds your name, email, address and so on. Pick one from a form's FormPilot menu to fill any form by matching each field to what it asks for.
